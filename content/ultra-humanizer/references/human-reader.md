# Reader, Truth, Proof

### 1. Uncomfortable Truth Block

**Move:** Put the worst fact in the plainest words. What happened, the number, who is accountable, the consequence, the plan. Never dissolve it into abstractions or agentless passives.

**Do:**
- Name the bad number first, in one short sentence.
- Name who answers for it (a person or a named team, not "management").
- State the consequence and the plan in the same block.
- Cut softeners ("temporary difficulties", "optimization", "challenging period").

Before:

> Due to temporary economic difficulties, an optimization of employment structure is planned for Q3.

After:

> We sold a third less than last year. If we cannot fix sales by autumn, we close the company or cut staff. I am responsible; here is the plan.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Неудобная правда» (chunk_01).

### 2. Brehnya Truth Ladder

**Move:** Climb down from the sweeping claim to the strongest rung you actually have. No rung available means cut the claim.

**Do:** Use this ladder, top to bottom, and stop at the first rung you can honestly stand on:
1. Found fact with source (statistics agency, poll with date).
2. Cited media or book you actually read (title plus what it says).
3. Documented personal observation (where, when, how many).
4. Honest minority view ("I might be the odd one, but...").
5. Nothing: cut it.

Before:

> Hardly anyone would argue that oil prices matter to Russians.

After:

> Per FOM, January 2016: 41 percent of Russians followed oil prices.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Как бороться с брехней» (chunk_04:109-330).

### 3. Consensus Trap Rule

**Move:** Decide what to do with every "everyone knows" claim. Known facts get cut; claims dressed as known facts get an owner.

**Do:**
- Fact the reader truly shares (water boils, smoking harms): cut it or compress to a clause. Never present shared knowledge as your insight.
- Claim presented as common knowledge: name who says so ("Cigarette makers assure us...", "The vendor claims...").
- When in doubt, attribute. An unattributed universal ("it is no secret that...") is always rewritten or cut.
- The flag side of this pattern (surface forms like "everyone knows", "it goes without saying") lives in slop-shapes.md.

Before:

> It is no secret that smoking makes men look more attractive.

After:

> Cigarette makers assure us that smoking makes men look more attractive.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Общеизвестное» (chunk_02:355).

### 4. Pull Quote Test

**Move:** Tear the line out of context, sign your name under it, read it cold. If it misleads alone, restore the scope inside the line.

**Do:**
- Imagine the sentence as a standalone quote with your name attached.
- If the clipped version says more (or worse) than you meant, add the missing scope: what exactly, for whom, under what condition.
- Prefer narrowing the verb or object over adding a disclaimer sentence.

Before:

> Minister: "Exams kill."

After:

> Minister: "Exams kill creative problem solving."

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Заработайте хорошую репутацию» (chunk_06:679-711).

### 5. Reader Task Lead

**Move:** Name the reader's decision or action first, then select, order, and word everything by that task. Run the two interchangeability tests.

**Do:**
- Open the section with the reader's task ("For allergy sufferers:", "To carry around the flat:").
- Keep only facts that change that decision; cut or demote the rest.
- Test 1 (neighbour business): would this benefit fit a competitor unchanged? If yes, it is filler. Rewrite until it names your specific fact.
- Test 2 (black marker): cover every product and brand name. If the text still fits anyone, it proves nothing. Restore the proper nouns and numbers.

Before:

> Our vacuum catalog is sorted by manufacturer and price. High suction power for a cleaner home.

After:

> For allergy sufferers: sealed filtration that traps fine dust, emptying without a dust cloud. To carry around the flat: a 5.2 kg body with a one hand grip.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Задачи читателя» (chunk_01:409); «Проверка черным маркером» (chunk_07).

### 6. Action Brief

**Move:** Every announcement or request carries four things: the ask, why now, what happens if missed, who to contact.

**Do:**
- State the exact act requested (what, where, by when).
- Give the deadline plus its reason (what happens at that hour, who uses the result).
- Say what happens if the reader misses it.
- Name a contact path for the exception case.

Before:

> Colleagues, please pay attention to work status in the system.

After:

> Close all completed tasks by end of day. At 20:00 we export the half year report; payroll uses it for bonuses. Cannot make it? Write to Mara today.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Знакомство» hot water letter (chunk_01:84-120).

### 7. Answer First Opening

**Move:** Open with the reader's answer, task, or hardest constraint. No chatbot framing, no roadmap sentence, no warm up paragraph.

**Do:**
- First sentence: the result, the answer, or the binding limit.
- Cut openers that describe the text ("In this article we will explore", "Let us take a look").
- If background is needed, it goes second, compressed to what changes the answer.

Before:

> In this article we will explore several approaches to choosing a laptop for study.

After:

> Buy 16 GB of memory; 8 GB stalls on video calls with slides open. Everything below follows from that.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Первое предложение абзаца» (chunk_05:513-599).

### 8. Evaluation to Fact

**Move:** Hunt every evaluative adjective and adverb, then replace each with factory, test, material, or measurement. Proof must decode against the reader's own life.

**Do:**
- Circle each evaluative word (high quality, unmatched, reliable, comfortable).
- Replace with: what it is made of, how it was tested, what was measured, under what condition.
- Decodability check: can the reader test this against prices, neighbours, or local scale? If not, translate the fact into their world or pick another fact.

Before:

> High quality assembly and unmatched durability for everyday use.

After:

> Micron fit parts keep dust and water out; the case survives a 5 metre drop onto concrete in our lab test.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Что делать с оценками», «Отказаться от оценок» (chunk_02:691-827).

### 9. Mechanism Plus Threshold

**Move:** Turn every product claim into material plus number plus exact switch off point. Claim without a threshold is still praise.

**Do:**
- Name the material or part doing the work.
- Give the number (load, distance, temperature, time).
- State the exact point where behavior changes (cuts off, shuts down, survives up to X).

Before:

> Durable and energy efficient charging dock.

After:

> Carbon fiber lid: drop an iron on it from 1.5 m and it survives. Charges to 100 percent, then cuts the dock so it draws nothing overnight.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Доказательства» (chunk_07:425-439).

### 10. Scenario Instead of Praise

**Move:** Replace praise with 2 or 3 concrete occasions: who, when, with what props, how often. The reader runs the film and concludes.

**Do:**
- Cast the scene: a named person type, a place, the props in hand.
- Include the usage moment plus frequency (daily timer, twice a week, each commute).
- Let the conclusion stay unstated; the film is the proof.
- Detail rule: every detail must serve the topic and carry a usage moment or frequency (see section S below).

Before:

> Powerful suction for a cleaner home.

After:

> Picks up cat and dog hair from rugs and skirting gaps. With three cats you would sweep twice a week; put it on a daily timer and the sofa stays clean.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Вместо оценок (истории)» (chunk_02:1011); «Детали и сценарии» (chunk_07:405).

### 11. Numbers in the Reader World

**Move:** Translate every figure into the reader's body, street, or wallet. A number the reader cannot feel is decoration.

**Do:**
- (a) Exact figure plus bodily referent: pair the abstract size with something the reader has held or entered ("60 m: a 17 storey building").
- (b) Round to useful precision and date it: kill "more than" and "approximately" as noise; give the measurement date for moving figures.
- (c) Cut impressive but useless metrics (cups of coffee, lines of code, raw server counts) unless the reader acts on them.
- (d) Watch adjacent number arithmetic: readers will combine nearby figures (25 percent over 4 years means what per year?). Check the math they will do.

Before:

> Diagonal 12.9 in, thickness 6.9 mm. Over 2 million lines of code ensure smooth operation.

After:

> 12.9 in: a bit bigger than A4, so PDFs show full page. 6.9 mm: like a 48 sheet sketch pad. Ships with the March firmware; battery figures below are measured on that build.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Числа», «Факты должны быть в мире читателя» (chunk_03, chunk_06:1079-1146).

### 12. Elastic Plural Rule

**Move:** Give every "various", "a range of", "in many cases" a count, names, a date, and a source. Or cut it.

**Do:**
- Replace the elastic word with: how many, which ones by name, when measured, who measured.
- One unnamed plural per claim is the limit; two means rewrite.
- If you cannot supply count plus source, delete the sentence. A vague majority claim is worse than silence.
- Surface flag forms live in slop-shapes.md.

Before:

> More and more citizens prefer domestic goods.

After:

> April 2016: 19 percent preferred domestic non food goods; 40 percent chose imports (FOM).

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Стоп-слова» (chunk_02:219).

## D1. Fill Mechanics: Surface, Never Invent

This section defines "fill" for every move above. Fill means surfacing buried source facts, never inventing substance.

**Allowed fill (surface what the source states):**
- Reorder: move the buried fact (threshold, date, owner, number) to the front.
- Unpack: expand a compressed source phrase into its stated parts (who, when, how many).
- Concretize: restate a source fact in the reader's units using stated values only (same number, plainer referent).

**Forbidden fill (substance change):**
- New numbers, dates, names, test results, or causes not present in the source.
- Upgrading a source hedge into certainty or a source guess into a mechanism.
- Importing a vivid detail from elsewhere because the scene "needs" one.

**Rule:** if the source does not state it and you cannot verify it, drop the claim or flag it to the user explicitly. A shorter honest text beats a vivid unverified one. Every Before to After pair in this file must be traceable to stated source content under this rule.

## S. Specificity That Serves the Topic

Port of v2 Specificity, constrained: concrete detail must serve the topic and carry a usage moment or frequency. Quirk without function is decoration (see human-structure.md for the garnish rule).

**Do:**
- Replace one abstract noun with a concrete, specific image drawn from the source.
- Attach the usage moment or frequency: when the reader meets this detail, how often, under what condition.
- Name names, give dates, use exact numbers when real and when the reader acts on them.
- Cut the odd detail that proves nothing, however charming. If it stays, it must carry a product fact.

Before:

> The office was noisy and distracting.

After:

> The office was loud during standup at ten: somebody two desks away crunched carrots through the whole call, so we moved reviews to the quiet room.

**Source:** v2 Specificity (SKILL.md); constrained by items 10 and 24b.
