### 1. Limits inside the pitch

**Move:** Volunteer what you cannot do, mid offer, kindly and specifically. Limits attract the readers you actually want.

**Do:** Name who you do not serve and in which situations you fail. Place the limit right after the matching promise, not in a footnote. State it plainly, without scorn for the refused party.

Before:

> We handle all accounting needs for businesses of any size.

After:

> We fix half finished books or set up clean accounting from zero. We are wrong for a CFO taking a company public. That work needs a Big Four team, so talk to Raketa first.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Ограничения» (chunk_07:801-823); backlog #25.

### 2. Limit to alternative

**Move:** Every refusal carries a route. Never leave the reader at a dead end.

**Do:** After each limit, give one concrete next step: a named competitor, a supplier, a pilot project, or an offer to help directly. Keep the alternative as specific as the refusal.

Before:

> We do not work with companies that resist new ideas.

After:

> We know new processes feel risky to adopt. So we start with a small pilot project. If our approach fits, we will gladly build something bigger together.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Ограничения» (chunk_07:815-823); backlog #25.

### 3. Care preemption and a named person

**Move:** Name the reader's worst case first. Admit what you cannot fix. Still offer something small and concrete. Replace institutional shields with a responsible human.

**Do:** Open with the anxious reader's situation in plain words. Say what is impossible. Then offer the partial help you can give (a call, freed up staff, a lesson). Sign with a name, a contact, and a plain consequence. Per D1, add only logistics already stated in the source (deadlines, contacts, documents). Never invent new services.

Before:

> Our team is here for all your event needs. The administration is not liable for delays.

After:

> Reading this a week before your wedding? Someone probably let you down. We cannot build a good wedding in a week, that is honest. Call us anyway. Sometimes our photographers and decorators free up, and we will think through what is still possible with you. Ask for Marina: +7 900 000 00 00.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Заботливо» (chunk_08:53-61); backlog #26.

### 4. Respect through care

**Move:** Respect is logistics, not ceremony. Show it with reasons, durations, costs, and a human sign off.

**Do:** Replace formal courtesy ("Dear passengers, we apologize for the inconvenience") with: why this happened, what happens next, how long it takes, what it costs, who answers questions. End with the exact next act (see entry 8).

Before:

> Dear clients! We apologize for any inconvenience. Contact us to reach your goals.

After:

> Your order will be ready Friday. Call before Thursday noon so we can schedule the bake. We cannot do same day cakes: the dough needs twelve hours to rest. Questions? Write to Oleg, he answers within two hours.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «С уважением, но без уважения» (chunk_03); backlog #27.

### 5. Genre pack for about and pitch texts

**Move:** In about, pitch, and application texts, swap boasting for work stories told to one specific reader.

**Do:** Apply four sub moves. (a) Target one reader: write to this company and this vacancy, in their words, naming their tasks. (b) Work story per client: say what you DID (shifts, hours, square meters, constraints), not whose logo you hold. (c) Credential to utility: every award or degree gets one sentence of reader benefit, or it is cut. (d) Admit the gap: state where you fall short plus your plan to close it. Per D1, use only facts already present in your history. Do not invent clients or numbers.

Before:

> I am a graduate of a top university, prize winner of national English contests, holder of Oxford certificates. I work with Sberbank, Gazprom and the Skolkovo technopark. I am confident I can handle any task.

After:

> I have taught English to children and adults since 2008. I know how to pull a school student up to exam level in four months, and how to keep an adult's working level with two sessions a week. I have not run social media campaigns, but I watched colleagues do it, so I will handle the technical part and ask for help on strategy at first. For Sberbank branches in Moscow I updated carpeting nightly, so nobody noticed a repair in the morning.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Откликаться прицельно», «Как рассказывать о клиентах», «Никогда не врать» (chunk_08:205-330, 579-615, 725-820); backlog #28.

### 6. No register cosplay

**Move:** Never imitate the reader's sociolect. Write as yourself, plainly, on equal terms.

**Do:** Delete styling aimed "at moms", "at schoolkids", "at businessmen", "at gentlemen". No diminutives, no fake premium diction, no borrowed slang. The partnership frame: you solve their problem honestly, they pay honestly. Respect includes their right to refuse you.

Before:

> Mommies, do you know about the new skincare line "BeautyVIP"? Our exclusive premium collection is presented for your kind attention!

After:

> New skincare line "Krasavella". For dry skin after pregnancy: fragrance free, ingredients listed on the label. If it does not suit you, say so and we refund within two weeks.

**Source:** Ilyakhov on respect and care tone (chunk_07:441-453); backlog #28b.

### 7. Taste veto

**Move:** After every rule, read the passage aloud. If it sounds mechanical, the edit failed. Rewrite, do not argue with the checklist.

**Do:** Read at speaking speed. Stumble, empty lungs, or synthetic flatness means rewrite with an example or a sensory detail. Rules serve sound: keep the impersonal where the actor is noise, name the actor where it adds color. Never sacrifice content for form. Per D1, enrichment means surfacing facts already in the source (reorder, unpack, concretize). New substance is dropped or flagged to the user, never invented.

Before (rule applied mechanically):

> Authors edit text in the new editor "Optima". They check it in the service and correct it in the same window. "Optima" preserves layout. The editor raises text quality thanks to the Focus mode. This mode helps raise concentration.

After (rewritten by ear):

> "Optima" is a text editor for people who write in plain style. Authors check text without leaving the window. Copy from Google Docs, paste, edit, paste into the letter: the layout survives. The Focus mode hides mail and chats so the editor stays on the text.

**Source:** Ilyakhov «Слушайте себя» (chunk_07:91-159); backlog #29.

### 8. Commercial endings: next act plus constraint plus deadline

**Move:** Commercial, about, and request texts close with a concrete next act, its constraint, and a real deadline. (Genre fork: essay and blog texts may stay unresolved or provisional; see human-structure.md for the essay-side catalogue. Commercial texts must not.)

**Do:** End with three parts in one or two short sentences. The exact act (call, write, send, bring). The constraint tied to a real cause (dough rests twelve hours, the report exports at 20:00). The deadline with its reason. No open questions, no "contact us to reach your goals".

Before:

> Contact us to reach your goals. We look forward to cooperating!

After:

> Call before Thursday noon and we bake it for Friday. Need it today? That is the one thing we cannot do: the dough needs twelve hours.

Second example, request text:

Before:

> Please pay attention to closing your tasks.

After:

> Close all completed tasks by end of day. At 20:00 we export the half year report, and payroll uses it for bonuses. Cannot make it? Tell us today.

**Source:** backlog #27 plus D4 genre fork decision; letter closings (chunk_08); action brief pattern (chunk_01:84-120).
