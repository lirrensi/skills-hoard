# Facebook Best Practices

## Platform Characteristics

- **Character limit**: 63,206 characters (practically unlimited)
- **Best for**: Communities, local businesses, older demographics, groups
- **Audience**: 25-55+, community-oriented
- **Tone**: Community-focused, conversational

## Post Types

### Short Post
Best for: Quick updates, questions, reactions

**Structure:**
1. Hook
2. Message
3. Optional CTA

**Length:** Keep under 3-4 lines for feed visibility

### Long Post
Best for: Stories, detailed content

**Structure:**
- Story structure (setup, conflict, resolution)
- Detailed insights with context

**Note:** Facebook truncates after ~3-4 lines in feed

### Video Post
Best for: Demonstrations, announcements, stories

**Tips:**
- Native video > link to external
- Upload directly to Facebook
- Add captions
- Front-load key info

### Community/Group Post
Best for: Building engagement in Groups

**Tips:**
- Ask questions
- Start discussions
- Share experiences
- Be genuinely helpful

## Best Practices

### What Works
- Facebook Groups (community building)
- Native video content
- Live video
- Local content and events
- Discussion-prompting questions
- Personal stories

### What Doesn't Work
- Links to external sites (major reach killer)
- Pure promotional content
- Ignoring comments
- Cross-posting from other platforms without adaptation

### Engagement Tips
- Ask open-ended questions
- Respond to all comments
- Use Facebook's native features (polls, reactions)
- Post at optimal times (1-4pm weekdays)
- Groups outperform Pages for organic reach

### Algorithm Notes
- Groups get priority over Pages
- Meaningful interactions > passive engagement
- Live videos get 3-5x more reach
- Native content > shared links

## Facebook vs. Other Platforms

| Aspect | Facebook | LinkedIn | Instagram |
|--------|----------|----------|-----------|
| Best for | Community, events | Professional | Visual |
| Tone | Casual, personal | Professional | Visual-first |
| Links | Avoid | In comments | In bio |
| Video | Native preferred | Optional | Reels |
| Groups | Strong | Not native | Not native |
