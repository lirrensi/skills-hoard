# TikTok Best Practices

## Platform Characteristics

- **Description limit**: 2,200 characters
- **Best for**: Brand awareness, viral potential, younger audiences
- **Audience**: 16-34, entertainment-focused
- **Tone**: Native, unpolished, entertaining + educational

## Post Types

### Short-Form Video (Under 60 sec)
Best for: Quick tips, hooks, demonstrations, reactions

**Structure:**
- Hook (0-3 sec): Pattern interrupt
- Content (3-45 sec): Core value
- CTA (final 5 sec): Follow, comment, share

### Long-Form Video (60 sec - 10 min)
Best for: Tutorials, stories, deep dives

**Structure:**
- Hook (0-3 sec): Must stop the scroll
- Setup (3-15 sec): Context
- Value (15 sec - 8 min): Main content
- CTA (final 30 sec): Clear action

## The "Hook" is Everything

TikTok's algorithm decides in the first 1-3 seconds whether to keep showing your video. Your hook must:
- Interrupt the scroll pattern
- State a clear benefit or curiosity
- Use visual + audio together

### Hook Formulas
- "You [mistake]? Stop."
- "The [adjective] way to [outcome]:"
- "[Number] things I wish I knew about [topic]"
- "POV: [situation]"
- "Wait for the end..."
- Question that creates curiosity gap

## Best Practices

### What Works
- Native, unpolished content (not over-produced)
- Trending sounds and formats
- Educational content in entertaining wrapper
- POV and day-in-the-life content
- Responding to comments with videos
- Duets and stitches
- Consistency (post daily if possible)

### What Doesn't Work
- Overly produced content
- Ignoring trends
- Hard selling/promotion
- Repurposed horizontal video
- Generic content

### Video Specs
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical (mandatory for Shorts/Reels)
- Resolution: 720p minimum, 1080p preferred
- Length: 15-60 sec optimal for reach, up to 10 min for retention
- Sound: On by default, must design for sound
- Captions: Required for accessibility and sound-off viewing

### Description/_caption
- 2,200 characters available
- 3-5 relevant hashtags
- First line matters for "For You" discovery
- Include keywords for searchability

### Engagement Tips
- Respond to comments with follow-up videos
- Use trending sounds (when appropriate)
- Post at optimal times (7-9am, 12-3pm, 7-11pm)
- Engagement in first hour helps algorithm
- Saves and shares > likes

## Algorithm Notes

TikTok explicitly weighs:
- Watch completion rate (higher = better)
- Rewatches (indicates value)
- Likes, comments, shares
- Profile visits
- Caption keywords for discovery

The hook directly impacts all of these — if people swipe away in 0.5 seconds, the algorithm deprioritizes your content.
