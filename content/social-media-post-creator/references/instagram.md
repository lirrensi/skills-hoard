# Instagram Best Practices

## Platform Characteristics

- **Caption limit**: 2,200 characters
- **Best for**: Visual brands, lifestyle, younger demographics, B2C
- **Audience**: 18-44, visual-first consumers
- **Tone**: Visual-first, engaging, often casual

## Post Types

### Photo Post
Best for: Single images, quotes, product shots, behind-the-scenes

**Structure:**
1. Hook (first ~125 chars visible in feed)
2. Body (story, context, value)
3. CTA (save, comment, share)

**Caption length:** 1,250-1,500 characters optimal

### Carousel
Best for: Step-by-step guides, lists, before/afters, comparisons

**Structure:**
- Slide 1: Hook/Title (make it compelling alone)
- Slides 2-9: One point per slide
- Slide 10: Summary + CTA

**Tips:**
- Each slide should work as standalone
- Use consistent visual branding
- Add text overlays for value

### Reel Script
Best for: Educational content, demonstrations, storytelling

**Structure:**
- Hook (0-2 sec): Pattern interrupt or bold claim
- Setup (2-5 sec): Context for the value
- Value (5-25 sec): The actual content
- CTA (25-30 sec): Follow, comment, share

**Tips:**
- Hook in first 1-2 seconds (this is when people swipe)
- Use trending sounds when appropriate
- Add captions for sound-off viewing
- Keep under 30 seconds for initial reach

### Story
Best for: Behind-the-scenes, ephemeral content, quick updates

**Structure:**
- Each frame: One idea
- Build narrative across frames
- Use stickers for engagement

**Tips:**
- Post daily for best presence
- Use all Story features (polls, questions, links)
- Build story arcs (beginning/middle/end for longer Stories)

## Best Practices

### The "Feed Truncation" Problem
Instagram truncates captions around 125 characters in feed. Your hook must:
- Be in the first line(s)
- Entice "more" tap
- Not be a wall of text

### Hook Formulas That Work
- Question: "Ever wondered...?"
- Bold claim: "The truth about [topic]"
- Numbered: "[X] ways to [outcome]:"
- Story: "So I almost [mistake]..."
- Result: "[Impressive result] — here's how:"

### What Works
- High-quality visuals (this is primary)
- Behind-the-scenes Stories
- Educational carousels
- User-generated content reposts
- Interactive Stories (polls, questions)
- Reels (heavily prioritized by algorithm)

### What Doesn't Work
- Low-quality images
- Too much text in images
- Ignoring Stories and Reels
- Only promotional content
- Generic content without personality

### Engagement Tips
- Reels get 2x reach of static posts
- Saves and shares > likes
- Use all Story features
- Respond to DMs and comments
- Consistency matters more than perfection

## Visual Guidelines

### Aspect Ratios
- Feed posts: 1:1 (square) or 4:5 (portrait)
- Stories: 9:16 (vertical)
- Reels: 9:16 (vertical)
- Landscape works but gets less visibility

### Image Best Practices
- High resolution (1080x1080 minimum)
- Consistent color palette/brand
- Minimal text overlay
- Visual hierarchy clear
