# Threads Best Practices

## Platform Characteristics

- **Character limit**: 500 characters standard, 10,000 with text attachment
- **Best for**: Conversation, casual chat, real-time engagement
- **Audience**: Meta ecosystem users, younger demographic
- **Tone**: Conversational, casual, authentic

## Post Types

### Short Post
Best for: Quick thoughts, reactions, questions

**Structure:**
1. Hook/first line
2. Core message
3. Optional CTA

**Length:** Under 500 characters

### Long-Form Attachment
Best for: Longer thoughts that need more space

**Structure:**
- Main post: Hook (500 char max)
- Attachment: Full text (up to 10,000 char)

**Note:** Treat the main post as the hook, the attachment as the depth. The main post still needs to sell the click/expand.

## Best Practices

### What Works
- Conversational, casual tone
- Direct engagement with others
- Reply-chain conversations
- Visual content (images, videos)
- Questions that invite discussion
- Less polished than LinkedIn, more human than Twitter

### What Doesn't Work
- Corporate/boring tone
- Hashtags (not typical on Threads)
- Link-heavy posts
- Being too promotional

### Engagement Tips
- Reply to builds thread visibility
- Engage with other creators
- Less frequency-obsessed than Twitter
- Authenticity over polish

### Compared to Other Platforms
- More conversational than LinkedIn
- Less compressed than Twitter
- Visual-first like Instagram
- No hashtags typical
- Follows Meta ecosystem patterns
