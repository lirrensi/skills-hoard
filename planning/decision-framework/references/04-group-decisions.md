# Group Decisions

Many hard decisions are not blocked by analysis. They are blocked by ownership, incentives, or value conflict.

## Step 1: Clarify Decision Authority

Name the roles:

- who decides
- who must be consulted
- who can veto on hard constraints
- who is merely informed

If no one knows who actually decides, fix that before adding more analysis.

## Step 2: Separate The Disagreement Type

Ask which kind of disagreement is present:

- `fact disagreement` - people believe different things are true
- `forecast disagreement` - people agree on facts but predict different outcomes
- `value disagreement` - people care about different criteria
- `ownership disagreement` - the process or authority is unclear

Each kind needs a different repair.

## Step 3: Repair By Type

### Fact disagreement

- identify the smallest evidence that could resolve it
- assign who will gather it and by when

### Forecast disagreement

- define the competing assumptions clearly
- use ranges, scenarios, or a pilot instead of arguing in abstractions

### Value disagreement

- make the criteria and weights explicit
- surface the trade-off instead of hiding it in vague language
- if needed, write the decision tenet the group wants to follow

### Ownership disagreement

- assign the decision owner
- define who gives input vs who commits
- prevent relitigation after the call

## Dissent Capture

For meaningful group decisions, record:

- the final choice
- the strongest objections
- why those objections did not carry the decision
- what evidence would vindicate the dissent later

This improves learning and lowers unproductive rehashing.

## Alignment Warning Signs

Stop and repair the process if:

- people keep changing the decision question midstream
- different stakeholders are using different success criteria
- analysis is being used to avoid naming a trade-off
- no one agrees on who can make the final call

## Output Pattern

Group decisions should usually add:

- decision owner
- stakeholder map
- dissent note
- follow-up trigger or review date
