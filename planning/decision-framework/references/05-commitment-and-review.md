# Commitment And Review

The point of the skill is not elegant analysis. The point is a decision the user can act on and revisit intelligently.

## Commitment Patterns

### Commit now

Use when the decision is sufficiently supported and reversible enough.

Include:

- chosen option
- short why
- first step

### Commit with guardrails

Use when the choice is right but risk needs monitoring.

Include:

- chosen option
- assumptions to watch
- signposts
- review trigger

### Pilot first

Use when a smaller action can cheaply reduce uncertainty.

Include:

- what the pilot is testing
- success and failure thresholds
- owner and timeline
- follow-up decision date

### Delay to learn

Use only when waiting creates meaningful information or preserves option value.

Good reasons to delay:

- known evidence is arriving soon
- the cost of waiting is low relative to the value of learning
- a pilot is impossible but a near-term event will reduce uncertainty

Bad reasons to delay:

- vague discomfort
- unclear ownership
- hoping conflict disappears by itself

### Do not proceed

Use when options fail must-haves, risks are unacceptable, or no path currently clears the bar.

## Review Triggers

Do not say `we will revisit later` without naming what later means.

Use:

- a date
- a metric crossing a threshold
- a risk signal appearing
- a new evidence event arriving

## Decision Log Rules

Always record:

- what was chosen
- why it was chosen
- what assumptions mattered most
- what would change the decision
- what happens next

This prevents hindsight rewriting and helps future learning.

## Anti-Patterns

- indefinite delay with no learning benefit
- `we can always change later` when reversal is actually costly
- documenting the logic so vaguely that no one can learn from it
